Doodling was just an escape from the boredom of long school days...

I started finding inspiration from the world and media around me...

Then I started seeing real artists online, seeing what they use and wanting to make art that was just as bright...

My parents saw how much I loved drawing and they got me my first drawing track tablet...

Relearning how to draw from paper to a track pad felt like I was starting over again. It was frustrating. I did not want to stop...

Then my friend commissioned me for a tattoo and suddenly art did not seem like such an impossible choice...

Art classes kept giving me more and more opportunities to keep creating...

To keep learning different mediums and tools...

I never stopped making and I kept getting inspired...

There was nothing I could not make come alive with my own two hands.
